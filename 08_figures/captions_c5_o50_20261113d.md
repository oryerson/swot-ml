Model: SWOT-GAT = C5 (two-seed mean, step 50,000). Scoring follows overlap50: a reservoir is scored in a window when gauge and model share ≥ 50% of the window's days; both series are centred over those days; NSE = 1 − Σ(S − O)²/ΣO² on the centred series; VR = model sd ÷ gauge sd; nRMSE = RMSE ÷ gauge range. High- and Medium-tier reservoirs only.

Excluded from every set that involves the rule models (the four-model sets, the rule ensemble, Fig 2b–c, Fig 3b, Fig 3d and Fig 4): 5 reservoirs where the published ISTARF-CONUS parameters leave ISTARF at capacity or empty on ≥ 95% of days under the GRADES inflow (DAM_ID 144, 1095, 1125, 1790, 1916). They stay in the SWOT-GAT-only panels (Fig 2a, Fig 3a, Fig 3c), whose scores they do not affect.

## Figure 2. SWOT-GAT and rule-model NSE over the full hindcast and the SWOT Period

(a) SWOT-GAT NSE over 1981–2020 at 419 reservoirs (median 0.32); dot size = capacity. Colours (YlGnBu 0.25–1.0) span the 10th–90th percentile (0.01 to 0.61); the bar's end triangles hold the 10% below and 10% above. Bonny (CO) has no row in sites_v5_g.csv; its location and capacity are GRanD's (gauges_expand candidates.csv).
(b) 1981–2020 and (c) SWOT Period: cumulative distributions of NSE by main purpose. Solid: SWOT-GAT. Dashed: the rule-model ensemble, the daily mean of GDROM, ISTARF and MODROM-cal storage on days all three have a value, scored like any model. Reservoirs where both are scored: 293 in 1981–2020 (the four-model set) and 267 in the SWOT Period. NSE below −1 is drawn at −1. The tables give n and the median NSE; reservoirs with another main purpose (10 and 8) are in the all-reservoir row only.
All reservoirs, median NSE SWOT-GAT / ensemble: 1981–2020 0.33 / 0.37 (NSE < 0 at 8% / 29%; ensemble higher at 47% of reservoirs); SWOT Period 0.77 / 0.22 (4% / 36% below 0; ensemble higher at 9%).

## Figure 3. Variability ratio of SWOT-GAT's 1981–2020 hindcast

(a) SWOT-GAT variability ratio over 1981–2020 at 419 reservoirs (median 0.48; below 1 at 92%); colours (viridis, reversed) on a log scale over the 10th–90th percentile (0.19–0.95).
(b) 1981–2020 NSE of the four models by thirds of storage irregularity (sd of the gauge around its fitted annual + semiannual harmonic ÷ the harmonic's sd, water years 1981–2020; tertile edges 1.52 and 3.21 over every reservoir with a value), on the 293 reservoirs all four models score over 1981–2020 (102, 109, 82 per third). Boxes: 25th–75th percentile, line median, whiskers 5th–95th, drawn at the axis limit beyond it.
(c) SWOT-GAT 1981–2020 variability ratio against the training variability ratio sd(SWOT storage, SWOT Period) ÷ sd(gauge, 1981–2020), the same gauge sd period as the VR denominator, so on the dashed 1:1 line the hindcast varies as much as SWOT observed; below it, less. Points coloured by SWOT-GAT 1981–2020 NSE on Fig 2a's scale. Spearman ρ = +0.86 [+0.81, +0.91] (block bootstrap), n = 401; partial ρ given irregularity +0.83; 85% of reservoirs below 1:1.
(d) Variability ratio distributions for SWOT-GAT and MODROM-cal: solid 1981–2020 (n = 293), dashed SWOT Period (n = 267). Medians SWOT-GAT 0.48 / 0.79; MODROM-cal 1.00 / 1.01.
